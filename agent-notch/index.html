Questions

Does it send my data anywhere?

No Agent Notch account and nothing sent to me. It reads the sign-in your agent CLIs already keep on your machine and asks each provider for your usage the same way the CLI does. The activity glow uses process and file timestamps only; it never reads your prompts or transcripts.

Does it work on Mac?

Windows today. The macOS port is in progress; the platform groundwork is already in the code.

Will the free version stay free?

Yes. The notch is MIT-licensed open source. Pro adds new features on top; nothing in Free gets taken away.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep Pro, with updates. The first 100 buyers pay $4.99; after that it's $8.99. One license works on two of your computers.

Where do I get Pro after buying?

Dodo Payments emails you a license key right after you pay; check spam or promotions if it hasn't arrived in a few minutes. Download the installer from the download page with your key and run it (Windows 10/11, no admin rights, nothing else to install). Then right-click the tray icon, choose "Enter license key..." and paste your key. Steps are in After you buy Pro. Stuck? Write to adityaryawork@gmail.com.

What happens to Pro if I'm offline?

Pro keeps working for 7 days without a connection. The license is checked once a day.

Can I get only Agent Notch alerts, and silence everything else?

Yes. The cyan halo and the done glow come from the notch itself, so they show even with Windows notifications off; only the toast and its sound need notifications. To keep just those: Settings, System, Notifications, turn on Do not disturb, then add Agent Notch under "Set priority notifications". Everything else stays quiet.
