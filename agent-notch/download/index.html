Download Agent Notch Pro

Paste the license key from your purchase email, then click Download. Works on Windows 10 and 11.

Your key is only sent to check it's valid. The download link works for 5 minutes.

After it downloads

  1. Run the installer. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is scanned on VirusTotal, and you can check the file yourself: SHA-256 6d39a1f63e999a8132e0db9251a0010b1804101bcec8b76f39e98112f9401052.
  2. Enter your key. Right-click the Agent Notch tray icon, choose "Enter license key...", and paste the key from your purchase email.
  3. Turn on the alerts. Open Settings and click "Install agent waiting hooks". Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and OpenCode can then tell the notch when they ask you something, need approval, or are done.
